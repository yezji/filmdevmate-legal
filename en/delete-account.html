Delete Your Account

FilmDevMate · OBJECTYE
You can delete your account here without installing the app.

Sign in below with your FilmDevMate account and your account and data are deleted right away. This is the same operation as My > Delete Account inside the app — it takes effect immediately rather than filing a request you have to wait on.

What gets deleted
  • Account details — email address, sign-in provider identifier, display name, profile
  • Development recipes you created and the reactions on them
  • Your development history and default process time settings
  • Development map territory records and film/developer addition requests
  • Exposure feedback data and photos submitted through the experimental feature
What is retained after deletion
  • Access logs we are legally required to keep — 3 months under the Korean Protection of Communications Secrets Act
  • Records of enquiries and dispute handling received by email — 3 years under the Korean E-Commerce Act
  • Anonymous statistics that can no longer be linked back to you, and records other users saved under their own accounts

See Article 3 of the Privacy Policy for the full retention schedule.

Deletion is permanent. Signing up again with the same email address will not restore your previous data.
